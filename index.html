The Full Details

KFC Background

The first KFC in Jamaica opened on June 18, 1975, at 170 Old Hope Road, Kingston 6. The Myers family opened it under a licence.

It is run by Restaurants of Jamaica (ROJ), which also operates Pizza Hut in Jamaica. As of January 2023 there were about 40 restaurants across 11 parishes.

Apart from the Colonel’s secret blend of 11 herbs and spices, ROJ says it uses Jamaican-sourced ingredients. It also does community work such as food bank donations and school programs.

Menu Highlights

  • Buckets: Family, Party and Big Bucket sizes for sharing.
  • Combos: Meal Deal, Big Deal and Big Six (chicken, a side and a drink).
  • Zinger: Spicy or BBQ.
  • Krispers, Hot Wings and Popcorn Chicken: the last in regular, BBQ and spicy.
  • Value picks: Chicken Box and Famous Bowl.
  • Sides: fries, biscuit, corn, coleslaw and mashed potato.
  • Drinks: Pepsi, Ting, Ginger Beer, Kola Champagne, Tropic Fruit Punch and Grape.

Prices are in Jamaican dollars (J$).

Pizza Hut Background

It is run by Restaurants of Jamaica (ROJ), the same company behind KFC Jamaica. It had 13 locations in late 2019. ROJ won “Pizza Hut Market of the Year” for Latin America and the Caribbean in 2019. The brand was founded in 1958 in Wichita, Kansas.

It offers dine-in, carry-out, delivery and online ordering. Kingston branches include New Kingston (57 Knutsford Blvd), Liguanea (4 Northside Dr) and Barbican (Kingston 6).

Jamaican twist: there is a Jerk Chicken Pizza, Jerk Chicken Alfredo pasta and Jerk Chicken Melts.

Menu Highlights

  • Pizza: Hawaiian, Pepperoni Lovers, BBQ Chicken, Vegetarian, Jerk Chicken, Supreme, Meat Lovers, Super Supreme.
  • Sides: Breadsticks, Cheesesticks, Cinnamon Sticks and Chicken Alfredo pasta.
  • Drinks: Pepsi, Ting, Ginger Beer, Kola Champagne, 7 Up and Grape.

Prices are in Jamaican dollars (J$).

Burger King Background

The first Burger King in Jamaica opened on December 17, 1984, on Main Street in Ocho Rios, St Ann.

It is run by Restaurant Associates Ltd (RAL), a Kingston-based franchise operator. It marked 40 years in 2025. RAL also operates Popeyes, Little Caesars and Krispy Kreme.

RAL had over 100 restaurants across Jamaica, Trinidad and Barbados as of January 2025. That count is for all its brands, not just Burger King. It opened its 32nd Jamaican location in December 2024, at Angels in St Catherine.

The Kingston-area branches mentioned include Barbican and New Kingston. The company says it plans more Jamaican flavours on the menu.

Menu Highlights

  • Burgers: Whopper with Cheese, Whopper Cheese and Bacon, Whopper Jnr with Cheese, Double Whopper with Cheese, Double Cheeseburger with Bacon.
  • Sides: Fries, Hershey Sundae Pie, Macadamia Nut Cookie, Chocolate Chip Cookie.
  • Drinks: Bottled water, fountain drinks, orange juice.
  • Add-ons: Pineapple, cheese slice.

Prices are in Jamaican dollars (J$).

Tastee Patties Background

Tastee has sold Jamaican patties since 1966. It was founded by Vincent and Valerie Chang, and the first location was at Union Square in downtown Kingston.

It later set up its headquarters in Cross Roads, Kingston (head office: 25A Half Way Tree Road, Kingston 5), and now has locations across the island. It is run by Tastee Limited and marked its 60th anniversary in 2026 with a free concert at Emancipation Park.

The Tastee Talent Contest (later the Tastee Talent Trail) helped launch artists like Beenie Man, Papa San and Mr Vegas. Nadine Sutherland won in 1979.

It also gives back: for example, 13 students each got J$60,000 toward tuition in a back-to-school initiative.

Menu Highlights

  • Patties: Beef Patty, Beef with Cheese Patty, Chicken Patty.
  • Breakfast: Ackee and Saltfish, Callaloo Breakfast, Curry Chicken Breakfast, Liver Breakfast, Salt Mackerel, Porridge.
  • Lunch and Combos: 1, 2 or 3 pc Chicken Combo, Baked Chicken Combo, Chicken Nugget Combo, Curried Mutton Combo, Stew Peas Combo Meal, Fries.
  • Soups: Chicken Soup, Red Peas Soup.

Prices are in Jamaican dollars (J$).

Domino’s Background

Domino’s has operated in Jamaica for more than 30 years.

It has about 17 to 18 stores (17 in November 2025, 18 in April 2026) across Kingston, St Andrew, St Catherine, Mandeville, May Pen, Ocho Rios and Montego Bay. It has over 200 team members. It offers carry-out and delivery.

Menu Highlights

Domino’s own site describes the menu as feast pizzas, chicken, breads, drinks and desserts.

  • Pizzas: Crunchy Thin Crust, Pan, Classic Hand Tossed, Domino’s Feast Pizzas, Jerk Chicken topping, Jerk Sausage topping.
  • Breads and Sides: Bread Sticks, Cheesy Bread, Cinna Stix, Chocolate Lava Crunch Cakes.
  • Drinks: Coca-Cola products.

Prices are in Jamaican dollars (J$).
