Top 5 Must Visit Fast FoodRestaurants in Jamaica

1. KFC takes the first spot on the list

KFC Jamaica Background

The first KFC in Jamaica opened on June 18, 1975, at 170 Old Hope Road, Kingston 6. The Myers family opened it under a licence. It is run by Restaurants of Jamaica (ROJ), which also operates Pizza Hut in Jamaica. As of January 2023 there were about 40 restaurants across 11 parishes. Apart from the Colonels secret blend of 11 herbs and spices, ROJ says it uses Jamaican-sourced ingredients. It also does community work such as food bank donations and school programs.

KFC Jamaica Menu Highlights

Buckets: Family, Party and Big Bucket sizes for sharing.

Combos: Meal Deal, Big Deal and Big Six (chicken, a side and a drink).

Zinger: Spicy or BBQ.

Krispers, Hot Wings and Popcorn Chicken: the last in regular, BBQ and spicy.

Value picks: Chicken Box and Famous Bowl.

Sides: fries, biscuit, corn, coleslaw and mashed potato.

Drinks: Pepsi, Ting, Ginger Beer, Kola Champagne, Tropic Fruit Punch and Grape.

2. Pizza Hut takes the second spot on the list

Pizza Hut Jamaica Background

It is run by Restaurants of Jamaica (ROJ), the same company behind KFC Jamaica. It had 13 locations in late 2019, so say “over a dozen” on your site since that count is old. ROJ won “Pizza Hut Market of the Year” for Latin America and the Caribbean in 2019. The brand was founded in 1958 in Wichita, Kansas. It offers dine-in, carry-out, delivery and online ordering. Kingston branches include New Kingston (57 Knutsford Blvd), Liguanea (4 Northside Dr) and Barbican (Kingston 6). Jamaican twist: there is a Jerk Chicken Pizza, Jerk Chicken Alfredo pasta and Jerk Chicken Melts. Drinks include Ting, Ginger Beer and Kola Champagne.

Pizza Hut Jamaica Menu Highlights

Pizza: Hawaiian, Pepperoni Lovers, BBQ Chicken, Vegetarian, Jerk Chicken, Supreme Meat Lovers, Super Supreme

Sides: Garlic Bread Sticks, Cinnamon Bread Sticks, Chicken Alfredo and more

Drinks: Pepsi, Ting, Ginger Beer, Kola Champagne, Tropic Fruit Punch and Grape.

3. Burger King takes the third spot on the list

Burger King Jamaica Background

Background The first Burger King in Jamaica opened on December 17, 1984, on Main Street in Ocho Rios, St Ann. It is run by Restaurant Associates Ltd (RAL), a Kingston-based franchise operator. It marked 40 years in 2025. RAL also operates Popeyes, Little Caesars and Krispy Kreme. RAL had over 100 restaurants across Jamaica, Trinidad and Barbados as of January 2025. That count is for all its brands, not just Burger King. It opened its 32nd Jamaican location in December 2024, at Angels in St Catherine. The Kingston-area branches mentioned include Barbican and New Kingston. The company says it plans more Jamaican flavours on the menu.

4. Tastees Patties takes the fourth spot on the list

Tastees Patties Background

Tastee has sold Jamaican patties since 1966. It was founded by Vincent and Valerie Chang, and the first location was at Union Square in downtown Kingston. It later set up its headquarters in Cross Roads, Kingston (head office: 25A Half Way Tree Road, Kingston 5), and now has locations across the island. I couldn’t find an official outlet count. It is run by Tastee Limited. It marked its 60th anniversary in 2026 with a free concert at Emancipation Park. The Tastee Talent Contest (later the Tastee Talent Trail) helped launch artists like Beenie Man, Papa San and Mr Vegas. Nadine Sutherland won in 1979. It also gives back: for example, 13 students each got J$60,000 toward tuition in a back-to-school initiative.

Tastees Patties Menu Highlights

Patties: Beef Patty, Beef with Cheese Patty, Chicken Patty

Breakfast: Ackee and Saltfish, Callaloo Breakfast, Curry Chicken Breakfast, Liver Breakfast, Salt Mackerel, Porridge

Lunch and Combos: 1, 2 or 3 pc Chicken Combo, Baked Chicken Combo, Chicken Nugget Combo, Curried Mutton Combo, Stew Peas Combo Meal, Fries

Soups: Chicken Soup, Red Peas Soup

5. Domino's takes the fifth spot on the list

Domino's Background

Domino’s has operated in Jamaica for more than 30 years. I couldn’t find an exact opening year. It has about 17 to 18 stores (17 in November 2025, 18 in April 2026) across Kingston, St Andrew, St Catherine, Mandeville, May Pen, Ocho Rios and Montego Bay. It has over 200 team members. It offers carry-out and delivery.
